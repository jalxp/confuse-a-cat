DRAWDOWN 391

An ordinary job where the numbers stop making sense.

A young technician covers for the senior watchman at a hydroelectric dam in the interior of Portugal, during a drought. He can't come in, so he talks her through the job by walkie. The job is a round of checks, repeated every night. The checks start to disagree with each other.

FIRST PERSON · ONE PLACE · NO WEAPONS · A VOICE ON THE WALKIE · RETRO STYLE

THE STUDIO

Confuse a Cat Games is a one-person indie studio, newly open. It makes small strange games, slowly, and shows the work while it happens.

The name is borrowed from a firm that staged small inexplicable events for an audience of one. I do the same for you.
